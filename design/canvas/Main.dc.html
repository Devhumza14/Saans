The problem

Aik number. Chaar zindagiyan.

Every winter Lahore’s air ranks among the worst in the world. Air quality apps show one number for everyone, but the risk depends on who is breathing it. Here is the same morning, AQI 287, for four people in one house:

  • A Ammi, 65 · asthma, heart condition Severe
  • A Ali, 27 · delivery rider, bike High
  • Z Zara, 8 · allergies, walks to school High
  • B Bilal, 34 · office job, car Moderate

Teen qadam

From one city reading to a plan for each person at home.

  1. 1

    Ghar banayein

    Pick your area of Lahore and add the people at home: age, conditions like asthma or pregnancy, and how they get around.

  2. 2

    Live hawa, har ghante

    Saans pulls live AQI, PM2.5 and wind for your area plus a 48-hour forecast, then scores each person’s risk with clear rules.

  3. 3

    Har shakhs ka plan

    AI turns those numbers into a short daily plan in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English, and alerts you before the air turns for someone at risk.

Under the hood

Numbers from data and rules. Words from AI.

A deterministic risk engine decides who is at risk and when. The language model never invents an AQI value: it only turns the engine’s output into a plan a grandmother can follow. Every answer is checked against a schema before you see it.

● open-meteo → air + weather, gulberg
● aq_snapshots → cache 15 min, fallback ready
◆ risk.engine → ammi: score 3.0 · severe
reasons: [age 60+, asthma, heart disease]
◆ bestWindows → 14:00–16:00 · avg 162
✦ llm.plan → zod ✓ · risk_level = engine ✓
✦ alert.agent → triggered by engine, worded by AI
✦ chat.agent → 5 tools, live numbers only
